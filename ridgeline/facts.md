# Ridgeline Veterinary Clinic: fact sheet

Fictional business for the Code2College Development Program. Every fact on a Ridgeline page must come from this file. If a fact is not here, ask; never guess.

* **Owner:** Dr. Elena Park, owner and lead veterinarian
* **Clinic:** small animal clinic, two veterinarians and four staff
* **Phone:** (555) 014 2290
* **Address:** 418 Quarry Road
* **After hours emergencies:** call the clinic number. The phone message gives the emergency number.

## What she asked for (her words)

> People call to ask if we're open and what we charge for a check-up, and both answers are on our site somewhere, they just can't find them. I want a site that answers the ten questions we get on the phone every day, and a way for people to request an appointment without calling.

## The ten questions the phone gets every day

1. **Are you open on Saturday?** Yes, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Closed Sunday.
2. **How much is a check up?** A wellness exam (check up) is $55.
3. **Do you take walk ins?** Only for nail trims, 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Everything else needs an appointment.
4. **What animals do you see?** Dogs and cats only. No birds, reptiles or farm animals.
5. **Do you handle emergencies?** During open hours, call us first and come right in. After hours, call the clinic number. The phone message gives the emergency number.
6. **How do I pay?** Cash or card at the front desk when you pick up your pet. No online payments.
7. **How do I make an appointment?** Call us at (555) 014 2290 during open hours.
8. **What do I bring to a first visit?** New clients are welcome. Bring your pet's old vet records (or ask your old vet to email them to us). Dogs on a leash, cats in a carrier.
9. **How do I refill a prescription?** Call at least one day ahead, then pick it up at the front desk.
10. **Do you do boarding or grooming?** No. We do nail trims only.

## Rules for the website

* No online payments. People pay at the front desk.
* Dental cleaning never shows a dollar amount. It says: Price depends on the pet. Call us.
* Appointment requests: for now the page says call to book. A request form would go to the front desk email, which needs the clinic's own account, so it waits until handover.
* Who reads it: mostly people on phones, many of them older. Big, plain, easy to read.
* Voice: warm and plain, like the front desk talking to you. No big words.
* Colors: green and white (the sign out front). Readability matters more than color.
